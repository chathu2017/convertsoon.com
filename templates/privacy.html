Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 25, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to ConvertSoon. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our global file conversion service. By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Files You Upload

When you use our service, you upload files specifically for the purpose of conversion. Regarding these files:

  • Temporary Processing: Files are stored only for the duration of the conversion process on secure ephemeral storage.
  • 15-Minute Auto-Deletion: We implement a strict retention policy. Files are automatically and permanently deleted immediately upon session closure or within 15 minutes of upload, whichever occurs first.
  • No Ownership: We do not claim any ownership of your content.
  • Confidentiality: Files are never accessed by humans, shared with third parties, or used for AI training.

Technical & Usage Data

To ensure service stability and security, we automatically collect:

  • IP address (processed via Cloudflare/Azure for security and region detection)
  • Browser type, version, and device operating system
  • Usage metrics (e.g., file types converted, timestamps) for performance optimization

Artificial Intelligence (AI) Privacy

ConvertSoon utilizes Azure OpenAI Service to enhance specific features (e.g., smart compression, OCR). We prioritize your data privacy in all AI operations:

  • No Training on User Data: Your uploaded files and content are NOT used to train, retrain, or improve our AI models or any third-party foundation models.
  • Ephemeral AI Processing: Data sent to AI endpoints is processed in memory and discarded immediately after the task is completed.
  • Purpose Limitation: AI is used solely to improve the quality and accuracy of the file conversion output.

Global Infrastructure & Data Routing

We utilize a high-performance global infrastructure to ensure speed and data sovereignty:

  • Regional Servers: Our infrastructure is distributed across major Regions in the USA, European Union (EU), and Singapore.
  • Smart DNS Routing: We use advanced DNS-level traffic management (via Microsoft Azure Services) to automatically route your request to the server closest to your physical location.
  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is secured using TLS/SSL encryption (HTTPS).

Advertising & Cookies

We allow third-party companies to serve ads and collect certain anonymous information when you visit our website.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Cookie Usage

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the site to function (e.g., maintaining your upload session).
  • Analytics & Ads: Used by partners like Google to analyze traffic and personalize content.

Third-Party Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party service providers to perform functions and provide services to us. These include:

  • Microsoft Azure & Azure OpenAI: For secure cloud hosting, AI processing, and temporary storage.
  • Cloudflare: For Content Delivery Network (CDN), DNS management, and DDoS protection.
  • Google Analytics & AdSense: For traffic analysis and advertising.

Security & Zero Tolerance Policy

We take the security of our infrastructure seriously. To protect our genuine users:

  • DDoS & Attack Prevention: Any attempt to compromise, flood, or exploit this website (SQL injection, XSS, botnet attacks) is monitored by our automated security systems.
  • Immediate Ban: Malicious traffic patterns result in an immediate, permanent IP ban at the DNS level.
  • Reporting: We reserve the right to report serious security violations to relevant Internet Service Providers (ISPs) and legal authorities.

GDPR & CCPA Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • GDPR (EU Users): You have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data. Given our strict 15-minute auto-deletion policy, user data is typically removed from our systems before a request can even be processed, ensuring your "Right to be Forgotten" is respected by default.
  • CCPA (California Users): We do not sell your personal information. You have the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If You are a parent or guardian and You are aware that Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
